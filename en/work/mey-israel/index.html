Overview

A logo for a water company — a drop and two waves wrapped around the Hebrew wordmark — and a public campaign built around one blunt message: “The toilet is not a trash can.”

An angry, overstuffed toilet character carries the message. The campaign runs in Hebrew and Russian across a billboard, flyers and a tri-fold brochure, all held together by the logo’s wave line.
