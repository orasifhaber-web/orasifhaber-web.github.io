Cosmix

Layered laser-cut wall art in sacred geometry

Client
Cosmix
Year

Discipline

Tools

Overview

Designs for Cosmix, a line of layered laser-cut wall art. Hexagons, stars and flower-of-life cores are drawn as stacked layers in wood, gold, silver and colour, so each piece reads as depth rather than a flat pattern.

The line also includes framed mirrors with Greek-key borders, presented in room settings alongside the brand’s logo.
