Codex Saltus

Cover artwork for the 25th anniversary compilation of StoneAge Records

Client
StoneAge Records
Year
2026
Discipline
Album cover, Typography
Tools
Photoshop, InDesign, Midjourney

Overview

Cover artwork for Codex Saltus — XXV Annorum Sum, the 25th anniversary compilation of StoneAge Records, a Swedish dark/psytrance label releasing since 2000. Ten tracks, thirteen international artists, out on Bandcamp, Beatport and Spotify.

The goal was to translate the music — spanning Forest, Psytrance and PsyDark — into a visual world connected to the meaning of Codex Saltus: “The Book of the Forest.”

View on Behance ↗

Concept

An ancient cosmic codex, born from the forest and holding its dark, mystical secrets. The entities rising from the book represent the secrets and energy held within it; the book itself is where the artwork’s whole world emerges from.

Typography

The typography combines the album’s ancient, mystical character with the StoneAge Records identity. Pirata One for the title — inspired by historical manuscripts, with a ritualistic presence. Tango Psychedelia for the subtitle — the typeface of the label’s logo, connecting the artwork to its identity.

Colour

Dark forest greens and earthy tones, combined with a luminous green glow — a natural, dark and mystical atmosphere with psychedelic touches.

Digital release

Designed for digital platforms, with a strong central composition and clear typography that stay recognisable at every screen size — from a phone’s now-playing view to a desktop player.
