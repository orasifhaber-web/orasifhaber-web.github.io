Trance Tribe

Brand identity for a psychedelic merchandise & fashion label

Client
Final project — Ariel University
Year
2024
Discipline
Brand identity, Merchandise, App & web
Tools
Photoshop, Illustrator, Midjourney

Overview

My final project in Visual Communication at Ariel University: a visual identity for a trance-event merchandise brand, including brand applications, products and an event app. It was presented at the program’s graduation exhibition.

A strict black-and-white system — a symbol built on a construction grid, hexagonal frames, geometric line patterns — gives each trance sub-genre room to carry its own character: PsyBient, PsyDark, Progressive, Hi-Tech, PsyTrance, PsyCore and more.
