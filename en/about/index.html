Since 2024 I’ve worked freelance, developing visual languages and campaigns for events, alongside music cover art and social media content. Most of that work lives in the psytrance scene — parties, crews, DJs and labels — where every event needs its own world and a long line-up still has to read at a glance.

I work directly with clients through the whole process: the brief, shaping the creative direction, design, revisions and final delivery. Repeat clients include StoneAge Records, a Swedish psytrance label, for whom I’ve designed a T-shirt, an event poster and the cover art for the album Codex Saltus — XXV Annorum Sum.

AI tools — Midjourney among them — are part of my workflow, alongside Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign.
